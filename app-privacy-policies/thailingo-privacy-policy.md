# Privacy Policy

## ThaiLingo

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

ThaiLingo is a Thai language learning application. Your privacy is important to us.

## Information We Collect

ThaiLingo does not require you to create an account or log in to use the app.

We do not collect or store personal information through ThaiLingo. The app does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal details.

## Data Storage and Sharing

ThaiLingo does not store personal information on our servers, and we do not sell, rent, or share personal information collected through the app.

The Google Play Store, your device, or the Android operating system may process technical information independently of ThaiLingo under their own policies. Please review the relevant policies for more information.

## Third-Party Services and Links

If ThaiLingo links to a third-party website or service, that service is governed by its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

## Children's Privacy

ThaiLingo does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users through the app.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app or its data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page, and the “Last updated” date will be revised accordingly.
